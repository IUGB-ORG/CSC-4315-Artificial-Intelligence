📚 Course Outline

These are invidual lecture notes for CSC 4315. This course provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence, starting with a two-rule test, a definition followed by a light historical survey. It proceeds with foundational Python and Mathematics concepts required for hands-on explorations sound understanding of Artificial Neural Networks. It then introduces Python Libraries and Tools required to tackle real-world AI models, discussing real-world Data and related challenges. It progresses to introduce Natural Language Processing, and concludes with getting the best out of Frontier Models, the so called Large Language Models and software agents built from these.
